# Social Content Playbook
### Free tips for TikTok, Instagram Reels & YouTube Shorts

*The top of the funnel. Goal of every post: deliver one useful, surprising money tip in 15–45 seconds, then point to the free checklist (which captures the email). Short-form vertical video is the channel — same clip cross-posts to all three platforms.*

---

## The strategy in 5 lines

1. **One idea per video.** Don't teach everything — tease one thing well.
2. **Hook in the first 2 seconds** or they scroll. Lead with the surprising claim.
3. **Every video ends with the same call to action:** "Free checklist — link in bio."
4. **Post consistently** (aim daily, or 4–5x/week). Volume + consistency beats perfection.
5. **The link in bio goes to the landing page** → they get the free checklist → you get their email → email sells the Playbook.

---

## Content pillars (rotate through these)

- **Fee outrage** — the hidden cost of advisors/fees (your strongest hook)
- **Tax reality** — the stuff nobody warned them about
- **Scam alerts** — protecting them from predators
- **Simple wins** — easy money moves anyone can do today
- **Myth-busting** — "you don't need a money manager" type takes
- **Story/cautionary** — broke-athlete lessons

## Posting cadence & workflow

- **Bank 20–30 videos before launching** so you never scramble for content.
- **Film in batches** — knock out 8–10 in one session in the same outfit/setting.
- **Post 1/day** across all three platforms (one film, three posts).
- **Pin your best fee-outrage video** to the top of each profile.
- **Bio link** always points to the landing page (free checklist).
- **Reply to every comment** early on — engagement teaches the algorithm to show you more.
- **Watch which hooks land**, then make 3 more versions of whatever pops.
