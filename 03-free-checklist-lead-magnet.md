## 🏈 The NIL Money Starter Checklist
**7 things to do with your NIL money before you spend a dollar**

*From The NIL Playbook — the plain-English money guide for college athletes.*

---

**☐ 1. Move 30% to taxes — immediately.**
Your NIL check is NOT all yours. Nobody withholds taxes for you. The day money lands, move 30% to a separate savings account and forget it exists. Spend it all and tax season becomes a nightmare.

**☐ 2. Wait 30 days before any big purchase.**
The excitement is the enemy. New money makes everyone want to level up their lifestyle. Make a rule: no big buys for 30 days after any deal. The hype fades; the money should stay.

**☐ 3. Open a high-yield savings account.**
A normal bank pays you almost nothing. A high-yield savings account pays real interest for the same safety. Put your tax money and emergency fund here. Takes 5 minutes online.

**☐ 4. Build a $1,000 starter emergency fund.**
Before investing a dime, stash $1,000 for life's surprises. It keeps one bad week from wrecking everything. Build toward 3–6 months of expenses over time.

**☐ 5. Kill any credit card debt.**
Card debt at 20%+ interest beats almost any investment you could make. Pay it off first — it's a guaranteed return you can't get anywhere else.

**☐ 6. Open a Roth IRA and buy a low-cost index fund.**
The boring secret to wealth: own a tiny piece of thousands of companies, cheaply, and don't touch it. A Roth IRA makes the growth tax-free forever. Starting young is a superpower.

**☐ 7. Learn the one question that saves you a fortune.**
Before you let ANYONE manage your money, ask: *"Are you a fee-only fiduciary, and how exactly do you get paid?"* A "small" 1% fee can cost you $300,000+ over your life. If their answer is fuzzy, walk away.
