# L23 — Next steps & staying consistent

**Lesson ID:** L23
**Module:** 6 — Your Year-One Plan

**Learning objective:** Cement the habits that make the plan work long-term, learn the 10-second gut-checks, and commit to consistency over perfection.

**Action step(s):** Save the 10-second gut-checks, pick your single next action today, and commit to consistency.

**Runtime estimate:** ~5 min

---

## Voiceover script

You made it. The last lesson. [pause] And here's the truth I've been building to this whole course: you don't need to be a finance genius. You really don't. You need to do a few simple things, avoid a few big mistakes, and — the part that actually matters most — **keep doing them.** Consistency is the whole game. Let me set you up to win it.

First, let me hand you a tool I want you to keep forever: the **ten-second gut-checks.** [pause] These are tiny questions you ask yourself in the moment, before you make a money move. Ten seconds each. They keep you on track without thinking hard.

**Before spending a new check**, ask: *"Did I move thirty percent to taxes? Have I waited thirty days?"* [pause]
**Before buying any investment**, ask: *"What's the expense ratio? Do I actually understand this?"* [pause]
**Before hiring anyone**, ask: *"Are they a fee-only fiduciary? How do they get paid, in dollars?"* [pause]
**And before *any* 'opportunity'**, ask: *"Is it guaranteed or urgent? Then it's probably a scam."* [pause]

Four little gut-checks. Screenshot them, keep them in your notes. They're this entire course, distilled into questions you can ask in a hallway.

Now — the real secret to making *any* of this work. [pause] It's not intensity. It's **consistency.** [pause] The athlete who invests fifty boring dollars a month, every month, for years, *crushes* the one who makes one big exciting move and then forgets about it. Wealth isn't built in a dramatic moment — it's built in the quiet, repeated, boring habit. The same way you don't get good at your sport in one practice. You get good with *reps.* Money is the exact same thing. [pause] Reps. Over time. That's it.

And let me take some pressure off, because this matters: **you don't have to be perfect.** [pause] You'll have a month where you spend too much. You'll forget a step. You'll see the market dip and feel your stomach drop. That's all normal. *None* of it ruins the plan. The only thing that actually ruins it is *quitting.* So if you slip — you don't quit, you just get back to the next rep. Consistency beats perfection every single time.

Let me bring the whole course home in one breath. [pause] Pay your taxes. Dodge the high fees. Keep it boring. Don't get scammed. [pause] Do those four things, *consistently*, and the money you're earning right now — right now, while you're young — can set you up for the rest of your life. Long after the games are over, long after the last deal, long after the cheering stops. That's the real prize. Not looking rich now. *Being* secure forever.

So here's your final move, and it's the most important one in the whole course. [pause] Don't just close this and feel motivated for an afternoon. Pick *one* thing — your single next action — and **do it today.** [pause] Maybe that's opening your separate tax account. Maybe it's opening the high-yield savings. Maybe it's finally opening that Roth IRA. Whatever step you're on from the last two lessons — do *that one*, today. Motivation fades. A completed action is forever.

You came in not knowing what to do with your first check. [pause] You're leaving with a full plan, the right habits, and the knowledge to protect every dollar. You're already ahead of almost everybody. Now go take that one action — and keep it boring. [pause] You've got this. I'm proud of you. Go.
