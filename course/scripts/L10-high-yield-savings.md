# L10 — High-yield savings accounts explained

**Lesson ID:** L10
**Module:** 3 — The Simple Foundation

**Learning objective:** Understand what a high-yield savings account (HYSA) is, how it differs from a big-bank account, and why it's the right home for tax and emergency money.

**Action step(s):** Open a high-yield savings account online and move your tax set-aside and emergency fund into it.

**Runtime estimate:** ~3 min

---

## Voiceover script

Quick one, but it's basically free money — so don't sleep on it. [pause] Let's talk about where your tax money and your emergency fund should actually *live.* The answer is a **high-yield savings account.** People call it an HYSA. Let me show you why it's a no-brainer.

Start with the problem. [pause] A regular savings account at a big, traditional bank? It pays you almost *nothing* in interest. Like, a fraction of a fraction of a percent. Your money just sits there, basically flat, while the bank does stuff with it. You're being too nice to your bank.

A **high-yield savings account** — usually offered by online banks — pays you *meaningfully* more interest. [pause] For literally the same money, doing nothing, just sitting in a different account.

And here's the part people get stuck on: *is it riskier?* [pause] No. That's the beautiful part. It is **just as safe.** Reputable high-yield savings accounts come with the same federal protection — it's called FDIC insurance — as your big bank. That insurance means your money's protected by the government up to a generous limit even if the bank itself failed. [pause] So: same safety, same easy access, *more* interest. There's genuinely no catch. Keeping your savings in a zero-percent account when this exists is just leaving free money on the table.

Let me show you the difference with a number. [pause] Say you've got five thousand dollars parked — maybe your tax set-aside plus your starter emergency fund. In a big-bank account paying basically nothing, you earn... a couple bucks a year. In a high-yield account, that same five thousand might earn you a *meaningfully* nicer chunk of interest over the year. Same five grand. Same safety. One just *pays* you for keeping it there. Why would you not?

Now — important — what is a high-yield savings account *for*? [pause] It's for your **emergency fund, your tax money, and short-term goals.** Safe storage that earns a little. [pause] What it is *not*: it is **not investing.** It's not where your money seriously *grows* — that's the next module, the index funds. The HYSA's job is to keep your safe money safe while paying you a bit better than zero. Different tool, different job.

So here's your move, and it takes about five minutes. [pause] Open a high-yield savings account online with a reputable bank. Then move your tax set-aside and your emergency fund into it. That's it. Your safe money is now finally earning its keep.

This is one of the easiest wins in the whole course. Knock it out, then meet me at the last foundation step: killing high-interest debt.
