# L00 — Welcome: How this course works

**Lesson ID:** L00
**Module:** 0 — Welcome

**Learning objective:** Understand what this course is (education, not advice), how it's structured, and how to get the most out of it.

**Action step(s):** By the end, the athlete has the course map in their head, knows the one-sentence promise, and is ready to start Module 1 today.

**Runtime estimate:** ~3 min

---

## Voiceover script

Hey. Quick question before we start. [pause]

You just got — or you're about to get — your first real NIL money. Maybe it's a five-hundred-dollar deal. Maybe it's fifteen thousand. Either way... what are you actually supposed to *do* with it?

Because nobody teaches this part. You learned the playbook for your sport. You did *not* get a playbook for the money. [pause]

That's what this is.

Welcome to The NIL Playbook. I'm your narrator, and I'm gonna walk you through every video in here like a teammate who already made the mistakes so you don't have to.

Here's the deal in one sentence: [pause] **Pay your taxes, dodge the high fees, keep it simple, don't get scammed, and let boring investments do the work.** That's the whole game. Everything else is just detail.

Let me show you how this course is built. [pause]

There are seven modules. We start with your **mindset** — because the first check is where most people fumble. Then **taxes**, because that's the part nobody warns you about and it's the part that can actually hurt you. Then the **simple foundation** — savings and debt. Then **investing** without getting ripped off. Then **protecting yourself** — scams, contracts, and the "friends with a deal" problem. And we finish with your **year-one plan**, mapped out by exactly how much you earn.

Each video is short. Three to eight minutes. And each one ends with one or two **action steps** — the actual thing to go *do*. If you only ever did the action steps, you'd already be ahead of ninety-five percent of athletes. I'm serious.

One important thing, and I'll say it at the end of every video too. [pause] This is **education, not advice.** I'm going to teach you how money works — how taxes work, how fees work, how investing works — so you can make smart calls and ask the right questions. I'm not telling *you* specifically what to do with *your* money. For that, you talk to a licensed pro, and I'll even show you how to find a good one who won't overcharge you.

Cool? [pause] Start with the next video. Let's keep your first check from becoming your first regret.
