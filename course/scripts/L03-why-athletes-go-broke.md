# L03 — Why athletes go broke — and why you won't

**Lesson ID:** L03
**Module:** 1 — Mindset: Don't Fumble the First Check

**Learning objective:** Learn the four real reasons athletes go broke and see how this course directly counters each one.

**Action step(s):** Identify which of the four traps you're personally most at risk for, and commit to the course module that fixes it.

**Runtime estimate:** ~5 min

---

## Voiceover script

You've heard the stat. A huge share of pro athletes are broke within a few years of retiring. [pause] People love to repeat it like it's some mystery. It's not a mystery. And once you see the four real reasons, you'll realize... none of them are about talent, and all of them are avoidable.

Here's the thing that surprises people: it's almost *never* because they didn't make enough money. [pause] These are people who made millions. They went broke anyway. So if it's not the amount — what is it?

Four reasons. Let's go through them, and I'll show you exactly how *you* dodge each one.

**Reason one: they spent like the money would never stop.** [pause] When checks keep coming, you start to believe they always will. So the spending scales up to match. Then the career ends — and the spending doesn't. *Your fix:* you already know the money is short-term. You've got the tool-not-trophy mindset and the 30-day rule. You're spending like an adult who knows the season ends.

**Reason two: they trusted the wrong people with it.** [pause] Athletes are surrounded — agents, "advisors," managers, family, a guy who knows a guy. Some are great. Some are quietly draining them or flat-out stealing. *Your fix:* Module 5 of this course is *all* about spotting the wrong people, reading contracts, and the one magic question that exposes anyone shady in about ten seconds.

**Reason three: they paid enormous fees and never noticed.** [pause] This is the silent one. A "small" one-percent fee every year, on everything you own, forever. Doesn't feel like much. Costs a fortune. *Your fix:* Module 4 breaks down exactly how that fee steals from you and how to invest for almost nothing instead. This single lesson can be worth hundreds of thousands of dollars to you. I'm not exaggerating.

**Reason four — and this is the saddest one. Nobody ever taught them the basics.** [pause] They weren't dumb. They were never *shown.* They got handed life-changing money and a stack of decisions, with zero education. *Your fix?* [pause] You're literally watching it right now. That's the whole point of this course.

So look — every reason athletes go broke, you're already disarming. [pause] You know the money's short. You'll know how to spot bad actors. You'll know how to dodge the fees. And you're getting the education nobody gave them.

Here's your move for this one. [pause] Be honest with yourself: which of those four are *you* most at risk for? Maybe you love to spend. Maybe you're too trusting. Maybe fees just sound boring so you tune out. Whatever it is — name it. Because the trap you can see is the trap you can beat.

That's the mindset module done. You've got the foundation. Now let's talk about the part that actually scares people: taxes.
