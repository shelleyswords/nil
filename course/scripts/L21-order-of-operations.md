# L21 — The order of operations

**Lesson ID:** L21
**Module:** 6 — Your Year-One Plan

**Learning objective:** Learn the universal 7-step order of operations that sequences everything in the course into one ordered checklist.

**Action step(s):** Write down the 7-step order of operations and identify which step you're currently on.

**Runtime estimate:** ~5 min

---

## Voiceover script

You've learned a *lot.* Taxes, savings, debt, investing, scams, contracts. [pause] Now I'm going to do the most important thing of all: put it in *order.* Because knowing all the pieces doesn't help if you don't know what to do *first.* So here it is — the universal order of operations. Seven steps. Same order for everybody. Do them top to bottom.

**Step one: set aside thirty percent for taxes — the day money arrives.** [pause] This is *always* first. Before anything else, before you even think about the money being yours, thirty percent goes to your separate tax account. Taxes come first because they're not optional and they're not yours. Lock this in and everything downstream gets easier.

**Step two: build a thousand-dollar starter emergency fund** — in your high-yield savings account. [pause] Before you invest a single dollar, you get that safety cushion in place. A thousand bucks between you and life's surprises. This is what keeps one bad week from blowing up your whole plan.

**Step three: pay off high-interest debt.** [pause] Those credit cards at twenty-plus percent? Kill them now. Remember — paying off a twenty-percent debt is a *guaranteed* twenty-percent return, and no investment beats that. So debt gets handled *before* investing. Always.

**Step four: grow that emergency fund toward three-to-six months of expenses.** [pause] Now you build the starter cushion up into a *real* one. Enough that you could lose every deal tomorrow and be fine for months. Take your time — this one builds gradually.

**Step five: open a Roth IRA and start automatic monthly investing into a low-cost index fund.** [pause] *Now* — only now, with taxes handled, a cushion in place, and high-interest debt dead — you start growing wealth. Open the Roth, automate the monthly contribution, buy the boring broad index fund. The machine starts running.

**Step six: keep learning, keep it boring, and don't touch the investments.** [pause] This step never really ends. You let the boring portfolio do its thing for years. No panic-selling, no chasing hot tips, no messing with it. Discipline *is* the strategy.

**Step seven: protect yourself** — the scam rules, the contract reviews, the magic question. [pause] This one runs in the *background* the entire time. Every new deal, every new "opportunity," every person asking for money — you run it through what you learned. Protection isn't a one-time step; it's a habit you keep forever.

So there's your whole course, in seven ordered steps. [pause] Taxes, starter emergency fund, kill high-interest debt, grow the emergency fund, open the Roth and invest, stay boring, and protect yourself throughout. *That* order matters — each step sets up the next.

Here's why the order is so powerful. [pause] Most people do this backwards. They start *investing*, or worse, *spending*, before they've handled taxes or built any cushion. Then one emergency or one tax bill knocks the whole thing over. You're not doing it backwards. You're building from the ground up, in the order that actually holds.

So your move: **write these seven steps down**, and be honest about **which one you're on right now.** [pause] Maybe you're on step one with your very first check. Maybe you've got taxes and savings handled and you're ready for the Roth. Wherever you are — name your step, and you know *exactly* what to do next. [pause]

Next lesson, we get even more specific — your plan tailored to *exactly* how much you earn. Let's go.
