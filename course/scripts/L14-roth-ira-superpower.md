# L14 — The Roth IRA superpower

**Lesson ID:** L14
**Module:** 4 — Investing Without Getting Ripped Off

**Learning objective:** Understand that a Roth IRA is a tax-free-growth bucket (not an investment itself), why it's ideal for young earners, and the basic limits.

**Action step(s):** Understand the Roth IRA: a bucket you open at a brokerage and buy index funds inside, where growth is tax-free forever.

**Runtime estimate:** ~4 min

---

## Voiceover script

So you know index funds are the *what.* Now let's talk about the *where* — because where you hold your investments changes how much tax you pay on all that growth. And there's one account that's basically a cheat code for someone your age. It's called a **Roth IRA**, and I want you to fall in love with it. [pause]

First, clear up the biggest confusion right away. [pause] A Roth IRA is **not an investment.** It's a *bucket.* A container. Think of it like a special account you open, and then you put investments — like your index fund from the last lesson — *inside* it. [pause] People get this mixed up all the time. You don't "buy a Roth IRA." You *open* one, then you buy index funds *within* it. Bucket on the outside, index fund on the inside. Got it? Good.

Now here's *why* this particular bucket is magic. [pause] With a Roth IRA, you put in money you've **already paid taxes on** — your normal, after-tax money. And in exchange... **all the growth comes out completely tax-free. Forever.** [pause]

Let me make sure that lands, because it's huge. [pause] You put in money you already paid tax on. It grows for decades. And when you eventually take it out — all those years of gains, all that compounding — you pay *zero* tax on any of it. Not a dollar. In a *regular* investment account, you'd owe taxes on those gains. In a Roth, the government takes its cut on the way *in*, and then never again. Decades of growth, totally tax-free on the way out.

And here's why this is an absolute *superpower* specifically for *you.* [pause] You're young. Which means your money has the *longest* possible time to grow before you touch it. The longer it grows, the more growth there is — and *all* of that growth is the tax-free part. So the younger you start a Roth, the more enormous the tax-free payoff becomes. [pause] Picture a few thousand dollars you put in at age twenty, growing for forty-plus years into a *much* bigger number — and you owe nothing on all that gain. That's the superpower. Time plus tax-free equals something genuinely massive.

A couple of practical guardrails. [pause] There's an annual *limit* on how much you can put in — it's a few thousand dollars a year, and that number changes over time. There's also an income limit, but here's the good news: **most college athletes fit comfortably under it.** So this account is almost certainly available to you.

Quick recap so it's locked in. [pause] A Roth IRA is a *bucket*, not an investment. You open it at a brokerage — that's next lesson. You fund it with already-taxed money. You buy index funds inside it. And every dollar of growth comes out tax-free, forever. For a young earner, that is one of the most powerful wealth tools that exists. [pause]

Next, we put it *all* together — Roth plus index fund — into your simple, boring, wins-quietly portfolio. Let's build it.
