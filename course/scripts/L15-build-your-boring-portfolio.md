# L15 — Build your boring portfolio (step by step)

**Lesson ID:** L15
**Module:** 4 — Investing Without Getting Ripped Off

**Learning objective:** Put it all together into the simple 4-step "boring portfolio" framework: open a Roth IRA, buy a broad low-cost index fund, automate contributions, don't touch it.

**Action step(s):** Open a Roth IRA at a low-cost brokerage, set up an automatic monthly contribution, buy a broad low-cost index fund inside it, and commit to never panic-selling.

**Runtime estimate:** ~5 min

---

## Voiceover script

This is the lesson where it all comes together. [pause] You've got the tool — the index fund. You've got the bucket — the Roth IRA. Now I'm going to hand you the actual step-by-step system that ties them into one simple, boring portfolio that quietly builds wealth for years. Four steps. That's the whole thing.

Real quick, before I give you the steps — a reminder. This is **general education, not a recommendation for your specific situation.** This is the widely-taught starting framework that millions of people use. Okay, here we go. [pause]

**Step one: open a Roth IRA at a low-cost brokerage.** [pause] A "brokerage" is just a company that holds your investment account — the big, well-known ones are great for this, and they charge *zero* dollars to open an account and zero to trade. You'll go to their website or app, click "open a Roth IRA," and follow the prompts. It's genuinely about as hard as opening a social media account. Don't let the fancy word scare you.

**Step two: inside that Roth, buy a broad, low-cost index fund.** [pause] Remember the last couple lessons — a total stock market fund or an S&P 500 fund. And here's where you use your fee superpower: **check the expense ratio and make sure it's under point-one-zero percent.** Cheap and broad. That's your core. You buy it *inside* the Roth bucket you just opened.

**Step three: add money regularly — automatically.** [pause] This is the secret sauce most people miss. You set up an **automatic monthly contribution** — even a small one — so a set amount moves into your Roth and buys your fund every single month, no matter what the market's doing. [pause] Why automatic? Because it removes *you* from the equation. No deciding, no forgetting, no trying to "time" it. The boring machine just runs in the background and builds your wealth while you live your life. Twenty-five bucks a month to start is totally fine — the *habit* matters more than the amount.

**Step four — and this might be the hardest one. Don't touch it.** [pause] Seriously. Don't check it every day. And whatever you do — **don't panic-sell when it dips.** Because it *will* dip. The market drops sometimes; that's completely normal, it's part of the deal. The people who lose are the ones who panic and sell low. The people who win just... leave it alone and keep contributing. [pause] There's a saying I want you to tattoo on your brain: **time *in* the market beats timing the market.** Staying invested through the ups and downs, for years, is what works.

That's it. That's the entire portfolio. [pause] Open the Roth, buy one broad cheap index fund inside it, automate a monthly contribution, and don't touch it. Do that *boring* thing for years, and it builds real, serious wealth. No stock-picking, no stress, no genius required.

So here's your move — and this is a big one, the action that makes you an actual investor. [pause] One: open a Roth IRA at a major low-cost brokerage. Two: set up an automatic monthly contribution, even if it's small. Three: inside the Roth, buy a broad low-cost index fund — then leave it alone. And four: make yourself a promise right now — *no panic-selling on a dip. Ever.* [pause]

Do those, and you are officially building wealth the smart, boring, winning way. Last investing lesson is next — what to *avoid* — so you don't undo all this good work. Let's finish strong.
