The NIL Money Starter Checklist

7 moves to make before you spend a dollar of NIL money.

Getting NIL money is exciting — but a few simple moves now protect it for life. Do these in order. Check each box as you go. No experience needed.
1

Move 30% to taxes — immediately

Your check is NOT all yours. Nobody withholds taxes on NIL money. The day it lands, move 30% into a separate savings account and forget it exists. Spend it all and tax season becomes a nightmare.

2

Wait 30 days before any big purchase

The excitement is the enemy. Make a rule: no big buys for 30 days after a deal. If you still want it after the hype fades, fine — usually you won't.

3

Open a high-yield savings account

A normal bank pays you almost nothing. A high-yield savings account pays real interest for the same safety. Keep your tax money and emergency fund here. Takes 5 minutes online.

4

Build a $1,000 starter emergency fund

Before investing a dime, stash $1,000 for life's surprises so one bad week can't wreck everything. Build toward 3–6 months of expenses over time.

5

Kill any credit card debt

Card debt at 20%+ interest beats almost any investment you could make. Pay it off first — it's a guaranteed return you can't get anywhere else.

6

Open a Roth IRA & buy a low-cost index fund

The boring secret to wealth: own a tiny piece of thousands of companies, cheaply, and don't touch it. A Roth IRA makes the growth tax-free forever. Starting young is a superpower.

7

Learn the one question that saves a fortune

Before you let ANYONE manage your money, ask: "Are you a fee-only fiduciary, and how exactly do you get paid?" A "small" 1% fee can cost you $300,000+ over your life. Fuzzy answer? Walk away.

You just did more than 95% of athletes ever do. 🙌
