# The NIL Game Plan — 30-Day Content Calendar
### One faceless short-form video per day, mapped to pillars + platforms

*This calendar pairs with `video-scripts.md` (40+ ready-to-produce scripts) and `posting-workflow.md` (how to turn one video into four posts). Every video is faceless: AI female voiceover + B-roll / AI-generated visuals / on-screen text. Same single clip cross-posts to all four platforms each day.*

---

## How to read this calendar

- **Pillar** — which of the 6 content pillars the video serves (rotate so no week feels repetitive).
- **Script #** — the matching script number in `video-scripts.md`. Scripts 1–20 already exist in `04-social-content.md`; this calendar uses the NEW scripts (21+) and re-runs a few proven older ones where noted.
- **Primary platform** — where this format performs best and where you post natively first.
- **Cross-post** — every video goes to all four (TikTok, YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels, Facebook Reels). "Primary" just means tailor the caption/hashtags there first.
- **Best post time** (ET, general starting points — adjust to your analytics): TikTok 6–9am & 7–11pm; IG Reels 11am–1pm & 7–9pm; YT Shorts 12–3pm & 9pm; Facebook 1–4pm.

## Weekly theme structure

Each week has a center of gravity so the month tells a story (mirrors the guide's order: taxes → fees → foundation → protection). Pillars still rotate daily — the theme just sets the "lead" topic.

| Week | Theme | Guide chapters | Goal |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Week 1 — "Don't fumble the first check"** | Mindset + Taxes | Ch. 1–2 | Hook new earners with the tax bombshell; establish trust |
| **Week 2 — "The fee trap"** | Fees + Myth-busting | Ch. 3, 5 | Drive the $400k wedge hard; the most shareable week |
| **Week 3 — "Build the base"** | Foundation + Simple wins | Ch. 4–5 | Give quick, doable actions; high save/share rate |
| **Week 4 — "Protect what you earn"** | Scams + Contracts + Cautionary | Ch. 6 | Protective content; convert trust into checklist sign-ups |
| **Days 29–30 — "Put it together"** | Recap + Year-one plan | Ch. 7–8 | Tie it together; push the checklist + Playbook |

**Each week's rhythm (repeats):** Mon = lead theme hook · Tue = Tax reality · Wed = Fee outrage · Thu = Simple win · Fri = Scam alert · Sat = Myth-bust or cautionary story · Sun = recap/round-up or "save this" listicle.

## Production notes

- **Batch by pillar, not by day.** Film/generate all of one week's voiceovers and visuals in a session (see `posting-workflow.md`). One narrator voice, one visual system = fast batching.
- **Pin a fee-outrage video** to the top of every profile (Day 8 "1% sounds tiny" is the strongest pin candidate).
- **Sunday = listicle/"save this" days.** These get the highest saves and shares — they're built for Facebook and the IG save button.
- **Every video ends with the same end card** and every caption carries *"Educational only, not financial advice."*
- **Re-run winners.** After 30 days, whichever 3–4 videos popped, make 2–3 new variations of each (new hook, same point). The calendar is a loop, not a one-time run.
