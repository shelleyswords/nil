**BODY**

Investing is the fun part. We'll get there tomorrow.

But if you skip the foundation, one bad week can wipe the whole thing out. So today is about the boring stuff that keeps you standing when life happens.

Two moves. That's it.

**1. Build a $1,000 starter emergency fund.**

A flight home. A busted phone. A surprise medical bill. Life happens to everyone. Without a cushion, you end up either going into debt or selling your investments at the worst possible time.

So before you invest a dime, stash **$1,000** somewhere safe and reachable. Over time, build it toward **3 to 6 months of your basic expenses**. This is your "life happens" money — separate from your tax account.

**2. Park it in a high-yield savings account (HYSA).**

Here's a free upgrade most athletes miss.

A normal big-bank savings account pays you almost nothing — like, pennies. A **high-yield savings account** (offered by lots of online banks) pays meaningfully more interest for the *exact same safety*. Same FDIC insurance protecting your money. Same easy access.

There's literally no reason to leave your emergency fund or your tax set-aside in an account paying 0%. Opening one takes about 5 minutes online.

To be clear: a HYSA is **not** investing. It's safe storage that earns you a little while it sits. That's exactly what you want for money you might need soon.

Quick gut check — got credit card debt? If you're carrying a balance at 20%+ interest, knock that out first. No investment reliably beats a guaranteed 20% return. Paying off that card *is* the best investment you can make.

So the order so far: taxes set aside → $1,000 cushion in a HYSA → kill high-interest debt.

Do those, and you've got a foundation most grown adults don't have.
