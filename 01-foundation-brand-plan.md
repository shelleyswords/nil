# NIL Money — Foundation & Brand Plan

*The strategic foundation. Every other deliverable (the guide, social content, landing page) is built on the decisions locked here.*

---

## 1. The one-sentence pitch

**A plain-English playbook that teaches college athletes how to keep, grow, and protect their NIL money — without handing 1–2% a year to an advisor who hasn't earned it.**

---

## 2. The problem we're solving

- NIL went live July 2021. There are now **500,000+ NCAA athletes**, and a large and growing share are getting real money for the first time in their lives.
- Most are **18–22, financially inexperienced**, and getting their first checks with no idea about taxes, fees, or investing.
- The vacuum has been filled by **"advisors," agents, and money managers** who charge 1–2% annual fees (or worse, sell commission products) — quietly draining tens or hundreds of thousands of dollars over a lifetime.
- **Nobody is teaching them the basics in their language**, on the platforms they actually use.

> **The hook that sells:** A 1% annual advisory fee doesn't sound like much. On money invested from age 20, it can cost **$300k–$500k+** by retirement. That single fact is our whole marketing wedge.

---

## 3. Who it's for (target customer)

**Primary: the mid-tier NIL earner — $5k to $50k/year.**

Why this group and not the superstars:
- It's the **largest** group by far (the $1M QB is rare; the $15k volleyball player with 80k Instagram followers is everywhere).
- They have **real money** — enough that fees and taxes matter — but **not enough** to attract a good advisor, so they get ignored or preyed on.
- They are the **most underserved and most reachable** through social media.
- A guide built for them **also serves** the top earners and the smaller earners — we just speak to the middle.

**Secondary audiences we'll naturally pick up:** parents of athletes, high-school recruits about to enter NIL, and even non-athlete young adults wanting the same plain-English money education.

---

## 4. The critical legal line (this shapes everything)

We sell **financial EDUCATION**, not **financial ADVICE**.

| We DO (education — legal, unlicensed) | We DON'T (advice — requires being a Registered Investment Advisor) |
|---|---|
| Explain how index funds, Roth IRAs, taxes, and fees work | Tell someone to buy a specific stock or fund |
| Teach what questions to ask an advisor | Manage anyone's money or make trades |
| Give frameworks and checklists | Give personalized "you should do X with your $30k" advice |
| Show general examples and math | Promise returns |

**Every piece of content carries a disclaimer:** *"This is educational content, not personalized financial, tax, or legal advice. Consult a licensed professional for your specific situation."*

This isn't a limitation — it's the safe, scalable, and honest way to build this. Bake it in from day one.

---

## 5. Brand & name

**Recommended name: `The NIL Game Plan`**

- "Playbook" is athlete-native language — it signals *a step-by-step plan you follow*, which is exactly the product.
- Clear, descriptive, and SEO-friendly (people will literally search "NIL money" and "NIL taxes").
- Works as a brand across the guide, the socials (@thenilgameplan), and the site.

**Strong alternates** (in case of trademark/handle conflicts):
- `Rookie Wealth` — broader, punchier, less tied to NIL specifically (good if you ever expand beyond athletes)
- `First Quarter` — sports + finance double meaning; more premium feel
- `Walk-On Wealth` — underdog energy, very athlete-coded

**Brand voice:** Confident teammate, not a suit. Talks *to* athletes, not down to them. Uses their language ("don't fumble your first check"), keeps it simple, never condescending, occasionally funny. Anti-jargon is the whole personality.

## 6. Product & pricing ladder

A three-rung ladder — each rung warms people up for the next:

| Rung | Product | Price | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Free** | Daily/weekly tips on TikTok, Instagram, YouTube Shorts + a free 1-page "NIL Money Starter Checklist" (email opt-in) | $0 | Build audience + trust, grow email list |
| **Core** | **The NIL Game Plan** — the full guide/course (PDF + optional video). The flagship product. | **$49 one-time** (launch price $29) | The main offer everything points to |
| **Premium (later)** | Templates pack, tax/quarterly-payment worksheets, private community, monthly "office hours" Q&A | $9–19/mo or $99/yr | Recurring revenue once audience exists |

**Why $49 one-time:** Low enough that a 19-year-old with one NIL deal buys without thinking; high enough to signal real value and fund ads. The $29 launch price creates urgency and early reviews. We can raise it as the brand grows.

**The free checklist is the engine.** It's the email-capture magnet — social drives people to it, the email list sells the guide on repeat. Without email capture, you're renting your audience from TikTok's algorithm.

---

## 7. How the pieces fit together (the funnel)

```
  SOCIAL (free tips)  →  FREE CHECKLIST (email opt-in)  →  THE PLAYBOOK ($49)  →  PREMIUM (subscription)
   top of funnel            capture                          core revenue            recurring revenue
   TikTok / IG / YT         landing page                     PDF + video             community
```

1. A 30-second TikTok ("The fee that's stealing your future") gets views.
2. Caption/bio sends them to the landing page for the **free** checklist.
3. They give their email → get the checklist → now we can reach them directly.
4. Email sequence + more content → they buy **The Game Plan**.
5. Buyers later upgrade to the **premium** community/tools.

---

## 8. The guide's content outline (preview — built out in deliverable #2)

1. **Don't fumble the first check** — mindset, the #1 mistakes, why "broke pro athletes" happen
2. **Taxes first** — NIL income is taxable, nobody withholds it, quarterly payments, set-aside rule of thumb
3. **The fee trap** — how 1% destroys wealth, advisor types, commissions vs. fee-only vs. DIY
4. **The simple foundation** — emergency fund, high-yield savings, kill debt
5. **Investing without getting ripped off** — index funds explained, Roth IRA, the boring portfolio that wins
6. **Protecting yourself** — scams, "friends" with deals, contracts, getting an LLC, when you actually need a pro
7. **Your year-one plan** — a literal step-by-step checklist by income level
8. **Resources** — vetted tools, what to ask any advisor, glossary
