Privacy Policy

Last updated June 2026

This policy explains what information The NIL Playbook collects and how we use it. We keep it simple because we collect very little.

What we collect

  • Email address — only if you choose to sign up for the free checklist or our newsletter. We use it to send you the resource you requested and occasional money-education emails.
  • Purchase information — if you buy a product, payment is processed securely by our payment provider (Stripe). We do not see or store your full card details.
  • Basic analytics — like most websites, we may use privacy-respecting analytics to understand which pages are popular (for example, page views and general location/device type). This is aggregate and not used to personally identify you.

How we use it

  • To deliver the resources, products, and emails you request.
  • To improve our content and understand what's useful.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

Third-party services

We rely on trusted third parties to run the site, including an email service provider (to send emails), a payment processor (Stripe, for purchases), and possibly an analytics provider. These services have their own privacy policies governing the data they handle.

Your choices

  • You can unsubscribe from emails at any time using the link in any email.
  • You can request that we delete the personal information we hold about you by emailing us.
  • You can use most of the site without providing any personal information at all.

Children

Our content is intended for college-age athletes and adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Contact

Questions or requests about your data? Email hello@thenilplaybook.com.
