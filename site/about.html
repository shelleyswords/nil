Our mission

Keep the money in the athlete's pocket.

The NIL Playbook exists for one reason: to give college athletes honest, plain-English money help — so the people who earned the money are the ones who keep it.

Why this exists

In 2021, college athletes finally won the right to earn money from their name, image, and likeness. Almost overnight, hundreds of thousands of young people started getting real money — often for the first time in their lives — with zero guidance on what to do with it.

Into that gap rushed a wave of "advisors," agents, and salespeople. Some are great. Many quietly charge fees that drain tens or even hundreds of thousands of dollars over a lifetime, or push products that pay them more than they help you. Almost nobody was teaching athletes the basics in language they'd actually use.

We think that's backwards. The person who earned the money should understand it — and keep it.

What we believe

  • Money help shouldn't be expensive or confusing. The core of smart money management is simple enough to teach anyone in plain English.
  • Boring wins. Pay your taxes, avoid big fees, invest simply, don't get scammed. That's most of the game.
  • No jargon, ever. If we use a term, we define it. If a number matters, we show a real example.
  • Education, not sales. We teach you how this works and what questions to ask — we don't manage your money or take a cut of it.

Our promise to you

Everything we make is built to be genuinely useful first. The free checklist and the Learn library will always be free. The full Playbook and course exist for people who want the complete step-by-step system — at a price that's the opposite of a 1%-forever advisor.

An important note on what we are — and aren'tThe NIL Playbook provides financial education. We are not a registered investment advisor, and nothing we publish is personalized financial, tax, or legal advice or a recommendation to buy any specific investment. We teach concepts and the right questions to ask, and we'll always point you to a licensed professional for decisions about your specific situation.
